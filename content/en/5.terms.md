These terms describe the basis for services, offers, and digital content of the company configured in the business profile.

## Scope

These terms apply to services, offers, and digital content of the company configured in the business profile unless a specific contract states otherwise.

## Services

Type, scope, duration, and prices of the services are defined by the relevant offer, contract, or service description. Changes or extensions should be documented in writing.

## Cooperation

Customers provide the information, content, and approvals required for delivery in time. Delays caused by missing cooperation can affect schedules and effort.

## Prices And Payment

Prices, payment terms, taxes, and expenses are based on the relevant offer or contract. Invoices are payable within the agreed period without deduction.

## Usage Rights

Usage rights to deliverables transfer only to the contractually agreed extent. Pre-existing tools, components, templates, and know-how remain with their respective rights holder unless expressly agreed otherwise.

## Applicable Law

Jurisdiction, consumer rights, warranty, liability, and termination rules follow the relevant contract and applicable law.
