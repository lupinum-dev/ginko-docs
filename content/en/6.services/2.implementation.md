The technical baseline provides the common foundation. In each project, business config, content, theme tokens, and page sections are adapted to the concrete company.

- German-first Nuxt i18n with English as the second locale
- Ginko Content for docs, blog, legal, services, and references
- Basin form flow with test endpoint during development
- Consent-gated loading for optional services
- Sitemap, robots, canonicals, and alternates
