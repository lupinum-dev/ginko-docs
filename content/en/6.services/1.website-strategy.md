## From goal to structure

A good business website starts with the decision which inquiries it should create. This service clarifies offer, audiences, page structure, primary CTAs, and tracking goals.

## Typical outcomes

- Page architecture for home, services, references, and contact
- Prioritized content for the most important decision questions
- Clear conversion paths for inquiry, appointment, or download
- Requirements for analytics, consent, forms, and SEO
