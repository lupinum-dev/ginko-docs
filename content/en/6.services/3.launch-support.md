## Check before going live

Before launch, content, legal pages, forms, consent, tracking events, sitemap, and mobile views are checked deliberately. The goal is a calm start without hidden production risk.

## Focus

- Form and Basin endpoints
- Analytics events without personal data
- Cookie and chat behavior by enabled services
- Sitemap, robots, canonicals, and language alternates
- short handover for editing and operations
