---

Hard-coded public URLs become stale when a locale translates a slug. Canonical link tokens keep authored content independent from its published path.

```md
[Install Ginko Docs]($docs/getting-started/installation)
[Explore navigation]($docs/essentials/navigation)
```

Those exact tokens are used in both language files. Ginko Content resolves them to English routes here and German routes in the translated page.

## Live links

- [Install Ginko Docs]($docs/getting-started/installation)
- [Explore navigation]($docs/essentials/navigation)
- [Open the rendering catalog]($docs/essentials/content-rendering)
- [Return to platform capabilities]($docs/essentials/platform-capabilities)

::alert{title="Authoring rule" type="info"}
Use canonical collection links for internal content. Use literal URLs only when the destination is intentionally locale-independent or external.
::
