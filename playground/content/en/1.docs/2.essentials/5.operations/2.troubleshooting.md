---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Diagnose content routes, package installations, and generated discovery output systematically.
icon: lucide:wrench
---

Start at the failing public surface and trace it back to its canonical owner. Avoid compensating in the UI for a route or locale defect owned by the content layer.

## A page returns 404

Confirm that the document belongs to a route-backed collection, is published, and appears in the generated content cache. Then compare its canonical identity, locale, content path, and projected public route.

## Installed behavior looks stale

Local tarballs can retain a package-manager cache entry when their version and path stay unchanged. Repair the lockfile or install from a uniquely identified artifact, then confirm the installed package contains the expected code.

## Discovery output disagrees

Search, sitemap, navigation, raw Markdown, and LLM output should consume the same canonical route facts. A disagreement normally indicates duplicated projection logic or stale generated output.
