`app/site.config.ts` is the single source for project-specific facts. For a new client project, adapt this file before editing components, the footer, legal pages, or tracking code.

## Required data

Replace at least these sections:

- `site`: name, description, URL, logo, and locales
- `identity`: legal name, brand, business type, country profile, register data, and management
- `contact`: email addresses, phone number, and address
- `legal`: jurisdiction, update date, and content responsibility
- `schema`: structured data and served regions

The config validates at import time. Missing required fields should fail early, not after the website has shipped.

The header language switcher uses `site.localeSwitcher`. `"dropdown"` is the default for business websites; `"segmented"` fits very compact two-locale headers. Language labels and flag icons come from the Nuxt i18n locale objects, not from a second header config.

## Environment variables

`.env.example` contains optional build-time values for the canonical URL, analytics IDs, Basin endpoints, and chatbot provider ID. These values flow into `siteConfig`; they do not replace the app's single canonical config.

For a project without tracking or chat, keep the optional services disabled in `app/site.config.ts`. The cookie banner will stay hidden.

## Optional services

Every non-essential service is disabled in the preset. Enable one only when the project actually uses it:

- `analytics.plausible`, `analytics.ga4`, `analytics.gtm`
- `marketing.metaPixel`, `marketing.linkedinInsight`
- `chat`
- `embeds.youtube`, `embeds.vimeo`, `embeds.googleMaps`, `embeds.calCom`, `embeds.calendly`

`app/config/service-registry.ts` derives the service inventory, consent requirements, and privacy service table from that config. Do not add a second service list in components, pages, or legal content.

Plausible, GA4, and GTM are prepared in `app/components/site/ServiceLoader.vue`. When one of these services is enabled in `siteConfig`, Nuxt Scripts loads it only after the matching cookie category is accepted. Components should still call only `useTracking()`, `useChat()`, or the form helpers, never provider globals directly.

Other analytics, marketing, or chat providers stay intentionally optional recipes. Add them only when a concrete project needs them, and attach them to the same service and consent source.

## Forms

Forms use `siteConfig.forms`. Development and test environments submit to `forms.testEndpoint`; production builds require explicit endpoints per form key.

Use `resolveFormEndpoint()` and `submitLeadForm()` from `app/lib/forms.ts`. Vue components should not contain Basin endpoints.

## Legal review

The included privacy, imprint, and terms pages are DACH-oriented placeholders. Before launch, review them against the actually enabled services, processors, countries, and contract terms.
