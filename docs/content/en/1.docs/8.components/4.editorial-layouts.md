## Fill the height beside text

<Layout gap="lg">
<Column size="md">
Honey is a natural product made by honeybees. It is stored in the hive and provides food, especially during bad weather and winter.

There are two main types of honey:

1. Blossom honey: Made from flower nectar.
2. Honeydew honey: Made from honeydew, a sugary liquid secreted by insects.

Honeybees store more honey than they normally need. This allows them to survive longer periods of unfavorable conditions.

## Nest layouts

An inner layout stacks its columns when its own space becomes too narrow.

<Layout gap="lg" surface="muted">
<Column size="lg">
Honey is a natural product made by honeybees. It is stored in the hive and provides food, especially during bad weather and winter.

<Layout type="border-dashed" gap="sm" stack="sm">
<Column>
### Blossom honey

Made from flower nectar.
</Column>

<Column>
### Honeydew honey

Made from honeydew secreted by insects.
</Column>
</Layout>
</Column>

</Layout>

## Options

| Property         | Behavior                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `layout.align`   | `start` (default), `center`, `end`: vertical alignment. Text stays left aligned.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| `layout.gap`     | `none`, `sm`, `md` (default), `lg`: space between columns.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `layout.stack`   | `sm`, `md` (default), `lg`: columns sit side by side from 32, 40, or 48 rem of available layout width. They stack below that width.                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| `layout.surface` | `default` (default), `muted`, `tint`: background based on the site colors.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| `column.size`    | `xs`, `sm`, `md` (default), `lg`, `xl`: relative width. `xs/xl` gives 1:3, `sm/lg` 1:2, `md/md` 1:1.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `column.align`   | Overrides vertical alignment for one column.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `column.media`   | `natural` (default) keeps the image ratio. `cover` fills the height beside text and crops the image. `contain` shows the entire image with possible empty space. Use for a column containing one Markdown image or one `figure`; figure captions remain below the image. Stacked Markdown images keep their natural ratio; figures use their authored `aspect`. |

Use `natural` or `contain` for logos, images with embedded labels, and photographs with important edges. Image zoom still opens the complete original.

For article flow, photo essays, wrapping, and margin notes, see [magazine layouts]($docs/components/magazine-layouts).
